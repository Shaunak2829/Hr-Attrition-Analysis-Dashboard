# 👥 HR Attrition Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## Short Description
A Power BI dashboard analyzing employee attrition across **1,470 employees**, identifying which roles, departments, income groups, and tenure bands are driving the highest employee exits — helping HR teams take targeted retention action.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Power BI Desktop** | Dashboard development & visualization |
| **Power Query Editor** | Data cleaning & transformation |
| **DAX** | KPI measures & calculated columns |
| **`.pbix`** | Development file format |
| **`.png`** | Dashboard preview exports |

---

## 📂 Data Source
- **Platform:** [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/)
- **Scope:** HR employee dataset covering job roles, departments, income, tenure, gender, and satisfaction scores

---

## ✨ Key Findings & Highlights

### 📊 Overview
- Total Employees: **1,470** | Total Attrition: **237** | Attrition Rate: **16.12%**
- Avg Tenure: **7.01 years** | Avg Monthly Income: **$6.50K**

### 🔍 Where Attrition is Happening
- **Sales Executives leave the most (57 exits)** — the single biggest attrition risk in the company
- **Research & Development loses 133 employees** — the highest attrition by department, nearly **1.5x the Sales department (92)**
- **Low income group drives the most exits (113)** — over **47% of all attrition** comes from the lowest earners
- **New joiners (0–2 years) account for 102 exits** — nearly **43% of total attrition** happens before employees complete 2 years

### 😟 Satisfaction Insight
- **Sales Executives and Research Scientists show the highest headcount at satisfaction level 1 & 2** (low satisfaction) — directly correlating with their high exit numbers

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- **Fix early attrition** — 43% of exits happen within 2 years; improve onboarding, mentoring, and early career growth paths
- **Review low-income band compensation** — 113 exits from the lowest earners signals a pay competitiveness problem
- **Prioritize Sales Executive retention** — highest exits (57) + low satisfaction scores; role clarity and incentive restructuring needed
- **Audit R&D department culture** — 133 exits from a single department points to a systemic issue beyond just pay
